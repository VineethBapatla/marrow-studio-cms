What we offer, and how we run a project.

Three core services, each shaped by the same process — listen first, sketch fast, build carefully.

Residential Architecture

New homes, extensions, and full renovations. We handle site analysis, planning approvals, structural coordination, and construction drawings — with a fixed set of design reviews built into the schedule so you always know what's coming next.

  • — Site & feasibility study
  • — Concept design & 3D walkthroughs
  • — Approval drawings & permitting support
  • — Construction oversight

Interior Design

Layouts, material palettes, lighting plans, and custom furniture — for new builds or existing spaces you're ready to rework. We source locally where we can and design joinery to be repaired, not replaced.

  • — Space planning & furniture layout
  • — Material & finish selection
  • — Custom joinery & furniture design
  • — Lighting design

Small Commercial Spaces

Cafés, studios, clinics, and small retail. We design for how the business actually operates day to day — staff flow, storage, and durability — while keeping the finished space calm and legible to customers.

  • — Operational & layout planning
  • — Brand-aligned interior design
  • — Vendor & contractor coordination
  • — Fit-out project management
Our process

From first call to handover

01

Conversation

A free, no-obligation call or site visit to understand your space, budget, and how you actually want to live or work in it.

02

Concept

We return with sketches, a rough material direction, and an honest budget range — before any detailed drawing begins.

03

Design development

Detailed drawings, material samples, and 3D walkthroughs, refined together over a set number of review sessions.

04

Build & handover

We coordinate contractors and visit site regularly through construction, ending with a walkthrough before you move in.

Questions

Common questions

How long does a typical project take?

A single room refresh can take 6–8 weeks. A full home renovation usually runs 4–8 months depending on scope and approvals. We give you a realistic timeline before any contract is signed.

Do you work outside Vijayawada?

Yes — we currently take on projects across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and parts of Tamil Nadu, with site visits scheduled around the project's phase.

What's your fee structure?

Fees are typically a percentage of construction cost for full architecture projects, or a flat fee for interior-only work. We'll give you a clear number after the first conversation, in writing.

Can you work with our own contractor?

Absolutely. We're happy to design and hand off drawings to a contractor you already trust, or manage the build ourselves — whichever fits your project.

Ready to sketch something out?

Tell us about your space and we'll get back to you within two business days.
